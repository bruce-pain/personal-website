## About

This very website! A deliberate exercise in minimalism. Built with [Astro](https://astro.build) for its zero-JS-by-default philosophy and [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com) v4 for styling.

## Features

- Dark mode with system preference detection
- Markdown-driven blog and project pages
- Mermaid diagram support in blog posts
